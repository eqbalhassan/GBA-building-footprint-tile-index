Contains data from OpenStreetMap (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL, and Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints, via GlobalBuildingAtlas (TUM Chair of Data Science in Earth Observation).

- **OpenStreetMap Data**: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0. To view a copy of this license, visit [https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) or [https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
- **Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints**: Sourced and processed as part of the GlobalBuildingAtlas initiative under ODbL terms.
- **GlobalBuildingAtlas (GBA.ODbLPolygon)**: Curated, structured into 5° x 5° GeoJSON tiles, and hosted by the Chair of Data Science in Earth Observation at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), led by Prof. Xiao Xiang Zhu.
  - Hugging Face Repository: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhu-xlab/GBA.ODbLPolygon](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhu-xlab/GBA.ODbLPolygon)
  - Full Dataset (mediaTUM): [https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/1782307](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/1782307)
  - GitHub Code & Docs: [https://github.com/zhu-xlab/GlobalBuildingAtlas](https://github.com/zhu-xlab/GlobalBuildingAtlas)
